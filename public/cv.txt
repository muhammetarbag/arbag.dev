PROJECTS (public source on GitHub, live demos in the browser)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Training labs, 9 in total, TypeScript, each with its own live demo:

[1] Aircraft Electrical Power Lab (ATA 24)
    115V AC / 28V DC bus topology, generator and bus-tie logic with interlocks,
    load shedding, and guided fault-diagnosis cases.
    https://muhammetarbag.github.io/aircraft-electrical-power-lab/

[2] Radio Navigation & ILS Lab (AEE 301)
    Localizer / glideslope DDM decoding and approach deviation tracking.
    https://muhammetarbag.github.io/radio-navigation-lab/

[3] Bleed Air & Pressurization Lab (ATA 21/36)
    Bleed, pack, cabin pressure and outflow valve model with a validation
    script and a quiz.
    https://muhammetarbag.github.io/bleed-air-pressurization-lab/

[4] Other labs: Autoflight (ATA 22), Hydraulic Power (ATA 29), Fuel System
    (ATA 28), Turbofan Engine, Four-Stroke Engine, Wind Tunnel.
    https://github.com/muhammetarbag

Browser tools on https://arbag.dev/lab/ :
    ARINC 429 word decoder, ADS-B / Mode-S (DF17) frame decoder with CRC-24
    check, tilt-wing VTOL transition model, rule-based QRH checklist prototype.

Other work:
    MHATv: plugin-based app for Samsung Tizen TV (private repository).
    ARK04: VTOL drone design prototype (CAD, packaging, power budget).
    Grunt vs. Judgment: design for LLM agents that gather data with
    deterministic scripts before any model call (design notes only; no
    published benchmark yet).
